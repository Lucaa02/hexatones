# HEXATONES
Hexatones was born from a simple idea: to break the synthesizer down into its fundamental modules—from power supplies to VCAs, ADSR envelopes, and even guitar pedals.

Full **flexibility** is the key to discovering entirely new sounds. By stacking, cascading, and inverting modules, or by relying on smaller, granular building blocks rather than rigid, all-in-one units, Hexatones empowers musicians to explore uncharted sonic territory. 

## Open Hardware

Everybody has the right to now why and how a sound is produced. 

## Analog is Key, Digital could help

Hexatones embraces the beautiful imperfections of **analog hardware**: circuit inaccuracies, thermal drift, and the subtle inductance introduced by wiring. We reject the dullness of perfectly predictable sound. Instead, the philosophy is to lean into the system, tune into its quirks, and turn those "errors" into features to create something genuinely **unique** every single time.

## Schematics, Layout, and Panelization
Keeping costs low is a core priority. Every design is optimized to leverage **affordable** PCB fabrication services and standard component sourcing.

Got ideas to trim costs even further? Smarter layouts, tighter panelization, or clever routing? The framework is completely open dive in and make it **better**.
